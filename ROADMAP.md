- Actual credential replacement and confirmed deletion were tested through HTTP with synthetic Linear responses and real persistence. The saved real connection was retained; manual checks covered masked/cleared fields, keyboard/focus, confirmation and cancel.
- S04 core live journeys passed for both adapters, including automatic/manual reports and selected refresh. ClickUp multi-assignee/TIML/archive cases and provider pagination/errors also passed synthetic transport/HTTP tests with real persistence; the single-member live workspace cannot establish those exact account variants. Models requested no live comments; separate real comment reads returned empty results.
- A non-failing frontend chunk warning of approximately 568 kB remains.

S01–S04 are complete under their combined contract/boundary and live checks above. S05–S06 still require separate implementation authorization; adapter acceptance does not complete their reliability/submission work.
